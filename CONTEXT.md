# Startup State 2

Government Opportunity Finder for the GOED bounty at AI Builder Day Part 2. One product: a founder describes a company and gets federal and Utah resources they should know about, with a why.

## Language

**Opportunity Map**:
The product. Ranked cards for a company: program, agency, value, deadline, why, concerns, similar awardees, next step. Not a geographic map.
_Avoid_: grant search, Navigator, Playbook, startup map

**Federal lane**:
Required core. Grants, SBIR/STTR, assistance listings, procurement from official APIs.
_Avoid_: every agency, the full federal government

**Utah lane**:
Optional advantage. Curated GOEO programs that help pursue a federal match, or that are the honest answer when federal is a poor fit. Not all 213 catalog rows.
_Avoid_: Playbook, every state program

**Source badge**:
Federal or Utah on each card.

**Fixture**:
One of the five official test companies in the brief. Case 5 may have no strong federal grant.
_Avoid_: Jordan/Maria/Marcus/Priya/David/Amir (those were Part 1)

craigcossairt/startup-state. Brand, infer, chips, and a Utah catalog subset may be copied. The five-surface app is not this product.
